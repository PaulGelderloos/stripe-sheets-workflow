How these numbers are made
Initiation
A HubSpot contact with an instruction date (initiatie_datum) inside the period and a course fee on record. A date alone also covers people who signed up and never paid; those are not counted.
Fee
cursusbedrag_betaald, which is recorded including 21% VAT. The excluding-VAT figure divides by 1.21. A fee of €0 is the second person on a couples tariff and still counts as one initiation.
Cents fix
A few fees were typed with the cents attached (62500 for €625). Where the raw value is absurd and value ÷ 100 is an exact tariff, the report corrects it and says how many it corrected.
Centre
From the place of instruction as typed by the teacher, mapped to the known centres (addresses and spelling variants included), falling back to the centre name on the record. An unknown place is shown as typed rather than hidden.
Teacher
Teachers are typed by hand in several fields and spellings. They are folded onto one name per person, by email address first and then by name.
Dates
Instruction dates are calendar days, so there is no time-zone shift. The end date is inclusive.
