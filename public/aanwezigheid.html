How these numbers are made
Population
HubSpot contacts whose intro talk date (lezing_datum_iso) falls inside the period and whose teacher has set an attendance status. Bookings with no status yet are not in any figure; their number is shown so a thin month reads as "not logged yet" rather than "nobody came".
Booked
All logged bookings, including cancellations.
Cancelled
Status Afgemeld.
Attended
Status Attended, plus Enquirer — someone who came along without booking.
No show
Status Not Attended: booked, did not cancel, did not come.
Signed up
The teacher ticked tm_course_enrolled at the talk. Paid is the harder fact: a course fee is on record. The two can differ, because payment often lands weeks later without the tick being revisited.
Attendance rate
Attended ÷ Booked. Cancellations stay in the denominator, as in the legacy report.
Channel
From leadsource_code via the Leadsource Codes sheet. Bookings made before 25 August 2026 carry no code and sit under "No code"; the channel split only says something from then on.
Centre
The centre on the booking, falling back to the teacher's own centre where the form left it blank. "Alle centra (online)" is the national online talk.
